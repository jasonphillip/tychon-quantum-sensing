**Gain of the best configuration over the baseline.** All three critics with the unanimous rule lower the false acceptance rate from 0.750 to 0.625 (27 to 10 false acceptances) and the accepted constructed edits from 16 to 4, at the cost of recall (0.750 to 0.500) and abstention (0.400 to 0.583). On the 14 uncontested report claims the same configuration reaches precision 0.800 (false acceptance 0.200), on very few rows.

## Reading

The strong local critic alone nearly halves the false acceptances of the two small critics (27 to 15) and never fails to parse, but it still accepts 6 of the 30 constructed false edits and 9 report claims that the key does not support. Requiring all three critics to agree gives the lowest false acceptance at the price of abstaining on more than half the rows; a majority vote is worse than the strong critic alone, because the two small critics say SUPPORTED to most rows (51 and 43 of 60), so two SUPPORTED votes are nearly automatic. No configuration is fit to accept claims unattended: even in the best configuration 62.5 % of the accepted claims are not supported by the key, and the 2-of-10 repeat flip rate says a single run of the strong critic is not stable either.
