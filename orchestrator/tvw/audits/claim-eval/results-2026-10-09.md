# Claim-checker evaluation — results 2026-10-09

**What this measures.** Two local critics (Ollama on 127.0.0.1: `qwen3-coder:30b`, `nemotron-mini:latest`; temperature 0.0) judge 60 claims through `critic_loop.py` with hybrid retrieval over the document and code corpora. Retrieval drops any chunk from the report under audit or its summaries (`--exclude-source Final_Report, final_report, summary, 01_deliverables`): 59 of 60 rows lost at least one such chunk, 453 chunks in total.

**Claim set** (`claim_eval_candidates.py`, seed 0): 30 original claims from `stage-4.7-claims-v31.jsonl` and 30 constructed edits of other originals — unit_error 8, revision_mismatch 8, unmeasured 7, causal 6, stale 1. The claims carry almost no version or date tokens, so the stale class has one row and its five-class accuracy means little.

**Labels are model-written**, under a one-time exception to TVW Hard rule 1 that Jason granted on 2026-10-09 for this candidate set only: labeler A = Codex `gpt-6-astra` (high effort), labeler B = Claude Fable 5.1, blind and independent. The critics under test stayed local. Labeler check on the 30 constructed rows: binary (supported vs not) 30/30 for both; five-class 0.833 (A) and 0.867 (B). On the 30 original claims the labelers agree on 15 (raw agreement 0.50; Cohen's kappa 0.283, below the 0.4 flag): A marks 7 supported, B 17. The 15 contested rows take the more cautious label, so only the 7 claims both labelers call supported count as supported. Every metric is reported for all rows and for uncontested rows only.

**Critic stability.** A repeat run on 10 rows changed 1 of 10 qwen3-coder verdicts and 0 of 10 nemotron-mini verdicts at temperature 0.

**Headline (all 60 rows, pooled).** Of the claims the two-critic ensemble accepts, 12.5 % are supported (false acceptance 0.875); the better single critic (nemotron-mini) reaches 0.140, so the ensemble gains 0.015 in false acceptance and loses 0.286 in recall. The critics accept many constructed edits — unit swaps, swapped numbers and causal overclaims. nemotron-mini fails to return a parsable verdict on 28 % of rows. On the 15 uncontested originals alone the ensemble does better (false acceptance 0.200 against 0.333 for nemotron-mini), on very few rows. With 7 supported claims in the set, every recall figure rests on 7 positives.


## Decision policy

Decision per row: accept iff every critic verdict is SUPPORTED; reject iff any CONTRADICTED or all UNSUPPORTED; else abstain; ERROR counts as parser failure and abstain.
