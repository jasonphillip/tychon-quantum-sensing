## Further comparisons and follow-on studies

- [Vector tracking confirmation](../workers/odmr-vector-tracking/docs/comparison_study_protocol_2026-10.md#addendum-2026-10-15-j-v2-confirmation-outcome-and-verdict). Classical exact inversion wins the realistic synthetic comparison; reliable tracking remains unproven.
- [Vector comparison: first decision](../workers/odmr-vector-tracking/docs/comparison_study_decision_2026-10.md). Original sealed-set decision.
- [Vector comparison: confirmation decision](../workers/odmr-vector-tracking/docs/comparison_study_decision_2026-10_v2.md). Second sealed-set decision.
- [Three-seed association-loss ablation](../workers/odmr-vector-tracking/docs/association_loss_multiseed.md). Removing association loss improved field estimates, but every arm still lost 108 of 120 field-cone sequences.
- [Known-bias vector models](../workers/odmr-edge-ml/docs/vector_v1_bias_field.md). Synthetic static estimation under a known bias; separate from tracking.
- [Measured-data advantage search](nv_advantage_search_2026-10.md). Two failures and one conditional calibration-efficiency pass on a single acquisition. No useful neural advantage was established.
- [Vector inversion development](nv_vector_development_2026-10.md). A stronger classical comparator reduced learned speedup from above 2× to 1.59×, below the registered gate. Candidate stopped.
- [Measured-data feasibility](../workers/odmr-edge-ml/docs/measured_data_feasibility_2026-10.md). Initial field-label eligibility assessment; later measured pilots evaluated different targets.
- [Training-target diagnosis](../workers/odmr-edge-ml/docs/training_target_diagnosis_2026-10.json). Recorded diagnosis of clipped training targets.
- [Claims requiring scrutiny](../orchestrator/INVENTORY.md#6-weakest-claims--what-would-not-survive-a-sharp-reviewer). Historical inventory; read its correction note and the later reports above.

These experiments extend beyond the original proposal scope.
[Readout, coupled-spin NV estimation, and a small QEC lookup](../experiments/cudaq-fpga/README.md) ran in FPGA fabric.
